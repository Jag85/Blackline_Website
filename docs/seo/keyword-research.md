# Keyword Research — Blackline Strategy Partners

**Source:** DataForSEO (Google, United States, English) · **Pulled:** 2026-06 ·
**Metrics:** `vol` = avg monthly US search volume · `KD` = keyword difficulty
(0–100, chance of ranking top-10) · `intent` = dominant search intent.

Scope: every indexable page **except individual blog posts** (those target
their own long-tail per article). Covers the 10 core pages + 4 location pages.

## Headline findings

1. **`business strategy consultant` — vol 1,600, KD 3, commercial.** The single
   best keyword we have: real volume, almost no competition, buyer intent. It's
   already the homepage title. Defend it.
2. **`strategy consultant` — vol 4,400, KD 7, commercial.** Highest-volume
   winnable head term. Weave it site-wide (home + services).
3. **`strategy consultation` — vol 4,400, KD 22, informational.** Big volume,
   under-used — a natural fit for the **Contact** page.
4. **`theory of constraints` — vol 3,600, KD 26, informational.** A whole blog
   topic waiting to be written (ties directly to the FOCUS/bottleneck framing).
5. **`business bottleneck` — vol 480, KD 5, informational.** Perfect for the
   **FOCUS Scorecard** page (it IS a bottleneck diagnostic).
6. **Location head term is `business consultant {city}`, not the strategy
   variant** — and **Dallas is the volume outlier (`business consultant dallas`
   = 720/mo)**, ~10× the other metros.

## What NOT to chase
- `business plan consultant` (2,900) — that's business-plan *writing*, wrong intent.
- `financial advisor`, `small business ideas`, `small business grants` — surfaced in expansion but off-offer.
- Branded tool names (FOCUS Scorecard, Clarity Index, Capital Conversion Compass) have ~0 search volume; they're brand assets, not acquisition keywords — always pair them with an informational qualifier (done in titles).
